# SolarNaija — Project Description

> **For**: Flippa listing, business valuation, investor pitch, or partnership discussions

## Executive Summary

**SolarNaija** is a content-driven affiliate website targeting the Nigerian solar equipment market. It provides research-based reviews, comparisons, and buying guides for solar inverters, batteries, panels, and complete kits — helping Nigerian homeowners and small businesses navigate a complex, rapidly-growing market.

### Key Metrics (Current)
- **Products Reviewed**: 9 (inverters, batteries, panels, charge controllers, solar kits)
- **Brands Covered**: 8 (Luminous, Felicity, Exide, Jinko, Rubitek, Blue Gate, Epsolar, Pylontech)
- **Buying Guides**: 5
- **Blog Posts**: 4
- **Categories**: 5
- **Legal Pages**: 4 (Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer, Methodology)
- **Tech Stack**: React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS
- **Bundle Size**: ~103KB gzipped (fast on Nigerian mobile networks)

## Market Opportunity

### The Problem
Nigeria faces chronic electricity challenges:
- Unreliable grid power (PHCN/NEPA) — frequent outages
- Rising fuel costs — petrol/diesel prices have surged
- Generator noise, fumes, and maintenance costs
- Confusing solar market — hundreds of products, fluctuating prices, quality varies widely
- Buyers need trustworthy information before making ₦500K–₦3M+ investments

### The Solution
SolarNaija provides:
- Honest, research-based product reviews
- Side-by-side comparisons
- Nigeria-specific buying guides (VA/KVA/watts explained, sizing calculators)
- Current Naira pricing (updated regularly)
- Clear recommendations for different use cases (1-bedroom flat, 3-bedroom home, shop, etc.)

### Market Size
- Nigeria's solar market is projected to reach **$2.5B by 2027**
- Over **60 million Nigerians** lack reliable grid access
- Growing middle class investing in home solar systems
- Small businesses (salons, shops, POS agents) increasingly adopting solar
